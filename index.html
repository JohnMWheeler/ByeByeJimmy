How the odds work

Inputs and assumptions behind the live forecast.

Forecast v1
01

Project scores

For this week, optimize each roster’s legal starting lineup using ESPN player projections, then blend that total with recent and season scoring. Later weeks use a recency-weighted team baseline.

02

Allow for upsets

Estimate score variation from completed league games. Shrink each team’s small sample toward the league spread; prior seasons will stabilize it when their data is connected.

03

Simulate the title

Run 30,000 versions of the remaining schedule and Bye-Bye Jimmy’s eight-team playoff bracket after its 14-week regular season, with no reseeding.

04

Price fairly

Convert forecast win and title probabilities to American odds without a sportsbook margin. Past-season backtesting is the next step for calibration.

Forecasts assume managers fill legal lineups and use independent team score draws. ESPN projections may change, and playoff tiebreakers are approximated by wins then points scored. These estimates are for league discussion, not wagering.
